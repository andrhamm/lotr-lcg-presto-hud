# Passage Through Mirkwood

The Core Set's introductory quest — teaches the core loop with minimal tricks.
First quest of the [[shadows-of-mirkwood|Shadows of Mirkwood]] cycle.

## Stages
| Stage | Quest pts | Advance / effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Flies and Spiders | **8** | no game text; place 8 progress |
| 2 · A Fork in the Road | **2** | clears the staging area; place 2 |
| 3A · Don't Leave the Path! | 0 | add a Spider from deck/discard to staging; **win = defeat Ungoliant's Spawn** |
| 3B · Beorn's Path | **10** | place 10; cannot advance while Ungoliant's Spawn is in play |

Stage 3 is a **random split** (3A vs 3B) — coin toss.

## Setup
- **Staging:** Old Forest Road (1 threat / 1 qp), Forest Spider (25 eng / 2 threat)
- No active location; nothing set aside. Does **not** scale with player count.

> [!tip] Companion value — preload
> Stage points are fixed: **1 = 8, 2 = 2, 3B = 10**. A quest picker could
> preload the stage/points so the player never types them. See [[quests|quest index]].

> [!warning] Threat / engagement watch (the app tracks threat)
> - **Hummerhorns** engage at **40** → deal 5 dmg to the engaged hero. Stay under 40.
> - **Ungoliant's Spawn**: 32 eng, When Revealed −1 willpower; 9 HP / 5 atk / 2 def boss.
> - **Chieftain Ufthak** (35 eng) snowballs +2 atk per resource token.

|---|---|---|---|

| King Spider | 20 | 1 | exhausts a character When Revealed |

## Locations
| Location | Threat | qp | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enchanted Stream | 2 | 2 | no card draw while active |
| Great Forest Web | 2 | 2 | exhaust a hero to travel |
| Mountains of Mirkwood | 2 | 3 | reveal a card to travel; search deck when explored |
| Old Forest Road | 1 | 1 | ready a character when traveled |
| Forest Gate | 2 | 4 | first player draws 2 when traveled |

## Treacheries
- **The Necromancer's Reach** (×3): each exhausted character takes 1 damage.
- **Caught in a Web**: attaches to highest-threat hero; can't ready unless 2 resources paid.
- **Driven by Shadow**: staging enemies/locations +1 threat; surges if none.
- **Eyes of the Forest**: discard all events from hand (removed on Easy).

## Notes
Split stage 3 gives replayability. Do NOT add Ungoliant's Spawn to the victory
display at stages 1–2 (loop risk at 3B). Next: [[journey-along-the-anduin]].
