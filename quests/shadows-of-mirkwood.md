# Shadows of Mirkwood

The game's **first cycle**. Core Set = the first 3 quests; the 6 Adventure Packs
add the rest. Overview MOC → [[quest-index]].

## Core Set quests (captured)
1. [[passage-through-mirkwood]] — intro; stages 8 / 2 / 10. Difficulty 1.
2. [[journey-along-the-anduin]] — Hill Troll; stages 8 / 16 / 0. Difficulty ~5.
3. [[escape-from-dol-guldur]] — prisoner + 3 objectives + Nazgûl; 9 / 15 / 7. Difficulty ~7.

## Adventure Pack quests (to capture)
- The Hunt for Gollum
- Conflict at the Carrock
- A Journey to Rhosgobel
- The Hills of Emyn Muil
- The Dead Marshes
- Return to Mirkwood

## Companion takeaways
- Stage quest-points are fixed per quest → a **quest picker could preload** them.
- Advancement is **not always progress-only** (combat/objective conditions).
- **Threat drives engagement** — quest enemy engagement costs matter (the HUD tracks threat).
