# Flight of the Stormcaller

First quest of the [[dream-chaser|Dream-chaser]] cycle and the first **chase
quest** — you race the Stormcaller across the Belegaer. Uses [[sailing-tests|Sailing]].

## Player stages
| Stage | Quest pts | Advance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Swift Departure | 8 | before the Stormcaller advances |
| 2 · Full Sail Ahead | 12 | complete, or deal 18 damage to the ship |
| 3 · The Weather Turns Foul | 18 | complete, or deal 18 damage |
| 4 · The Cape of Andrast | 24 | complete, or deal 18 damage |

## The chase mechanic (a second progress track)
- The **Stormcaller has its own quest deck**: 2C/D (12), 3C/D (18), 4C/D (24),
  with a head start.
- Each round it **advances by its threat + the threat of the top encounter card**
  discarded. **Off-course → +2 threat** (nearly doubles its pace).
- **Loss:** if the Stormcaller finishes 4C/D before you complete your quest or
  sink the ship, you lose.
- Damage caps: stage 2 = 4/round, stage 3 = 8/round, stage 4 = none.

> [!tip] Companion opportunity
> This quest has **two progress tracks** (yours + the enemy's). The HUD's
> progress row could optionally show an enemy/chase track for chase quests.

## Sailing factors in
Mandatory [[sailing-tests|sailing test]] each quest phase. Off-course penalties:
- Stage 1: move **2 progress from your quest to the Stormcaller's**.
- Stage 3: reveal an extra encounter card.
- Stages 2–3: Stormcaller **+2 threat**.

## Ship-enemies
- **Stormcaller**: 6 atk / 6 def / 18 HP; puts 2 Corsairs into play when engaged;
  only ship-objectives can block it.
- **Swift Raider** (stage 2): engages anyone committing 4+ characters; brings 2 Corsairs.
- **Corsair Skiff**: Surges; choose Boarding 1 or +3 threat.

## Locations
- **Vast Coastland** (6 threat): sits in the Stormcaller's area until you catch up.
- **Hidden Cove** (3/3): heals 4 to all ship-objectives; travel costs the Stormcaller 2 progress.
- **Jagged Reef** (1 threat): 2 dmg to the fleet if off-course; self-explores while on-course.

## Notes
Two win paths: out-quest the ship, or sink it (18 dmg). Off-course is the tension
lever. Cycle: [[dream-chaser]].
