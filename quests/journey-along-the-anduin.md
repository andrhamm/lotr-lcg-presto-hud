# Journey Along the Anduin

Core quest 2 — a sharp difficulty jump. The Hill Troll and threat management
dominate. Part of [[shadows-of-mirkwood|Shadows of Mirkwood]].

## Stages
| Stage | Quest pts | Advance / effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · To the River | **8** | place 8 **AND defeat the Hill Troll** |
| 2 · Anduin Passage | **16** | reveal 1 **extra** card each quest phase; enemies don't auto-engage (optional only); place 16 |
| 3 · Ambush on the Shore | 0 | reveal 2/player on entry; **no staging step**; win = **defeat all enemies** |

> [!note] Quests bend the phase flow
> Stage 1/3 add **combat win conditions** (not progress-only). Stage 2 adds an
> extra staging reveal and disables auto-engagement. Stage 3 skips staging.
> A companion could show these per-quest modifiers on the relevant phase screen.

## Setup
- Reveal cards = player count into staging.
- Search + add **Hill Troll** to staging (1 threat, 30 eng, 9 HP / 6 atk / 3 def;
  excess damage → threat increase).
- No active location initially.

> [!warning] Threat thresholds (app tracks threat)
> - **Start under 30** — Hill Troll engages at 30.
> - **35** trips treacheries (Evil Storm deals 1 to all at ≥35).
> - **Goblin Sniper** (48 eng) can't be optionally engaged while others in staging.

## Enemies
| Enemy | Eng | Threat | HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hill Troll | 30 | 1 | 9 | 6 atk / 3 def; excess dmg → +threat |
| Goblin Sniper | 48 | 2 | 2 | no optional engage while others staged |
| Marsh Adder | 40 | 3 | 7 | 4 atk; raises threat when attacking |
| Wargs | 20 | 2 | 4 | returns to staging if shadow blank |
| Chieftain Ufthak | 35 | 2 | 6 | +2 atk per resource |

## Locations
| Location | Threat | qp | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brown Lands | 5 | 1 | high staging threat |
| East Bight | 1 | 6 | forces travel if none active |
| Gladden Fields | 3 | 3 | +1 threat all at round end while active (3 victory) |

## Treacheries
- **Evil Storm**: nothing at ≤34; 1 dmg to all at ≥35.
- **Necromancer's Reach**: each exhausted character takes 1.
- **Massing at Night**: reveal 1 extra card/player.

## Notes
Build board early, hold threat down to delay the Troll. Next: [[escape-from-dol-guldur]].
