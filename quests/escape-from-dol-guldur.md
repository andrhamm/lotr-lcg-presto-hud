# Escape from Dol Guldur

The Core Set's boss quest — captured hero, 3 objectives, the Nazgul. Brutal
solo, easier multiplayer. Part of [[shadows-of-mirkwood|Shadows of Mirkwood]].

## Stages
| Stage | Quest pts | Advance condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The Necromancer's Tower | **9** | 9 progress **AND control ≥ 1 objective** |
| 2 · Through the Caverns | **15** | 15 progress **AND control all 3 objectives**; may rescue the prisoner (Nazgûl enters staging, 5 threat) |
| 3 · Out of the Dungeons | **7** | 7 progress; win after the **Nazgûl is defeated** |

> [!note] Advancement isn't progress-only
> Objective control gates stages 1–2. The companion's stage-complete flow
> should allow "not yet — objective/enemy condition unmet."

## Unusual setup
- One **random hero is the prisoner**: can't quest/attack/defend/collect resources
  until rescued at stage 2. Scaling favors multiplayer.
- Reveal 3 encounter cards; resolve **Guarded** on each objective.
- **Ally restriction (stages 1–2):** only 1 ally played per round as a group.

## Objectives (all Guarded + Restricted)
- **Gandalf's Map** — attached hero can't attack/defend.
- **Dungeon Torch** — **+2 threat/round** to attached hero (app tracks threat!).
- **Shadow Key** — 1 dmg/round to attached hero (negated by Mithril Shirt).

## Boss
- **Nazgûl of Dol Guldur**: enters stage 2, 5 threat in staging. 4 atk / 3 def / 9 HP.
  Discard 1 character per shadow effect resolved. Immune to attachments.

## Notable encounter cards
- **Hummerhorns** (40 eng): 5 dmg on engage.
- **Ungoliant's Spawn** (32 eng, 9 HP): −1 wp When Revealed.
- **Dungeon Jailor** (38 eng): reshuffles unclaimed objectives if quest fails with it staged.
- **Enchanted Stream**: no card draw while active.
- **The Necromancer's Reach** (×3), **Caught in a Web** (×2), **Iron Shackles**.

## Notes
Willpower allies early; delay claiming harmful objectives; dedicate defense to
the Nazgûl. This is the cycle's finale-in-miniature; the [[shadows-of-mirkwood|cycle]]
adventure packs continue the story. Sailing arrives later — see [[dream-chaser|Dream-chaser]].
